Activity 1: Drink Coaster

We were tasked with creating a drink coaster design for a character of our choice. Me and my partner chose Genghis Khan, and with the help of AI we had it simulate and personate Genghis Khan so we could learn more about him personally and create a coaster design based off those answers. With the help of generative AI we created an image of this said coaster to reflect on who he was as a person.
